Privacy Policy

BeeKindBeeYourself Shop Analytics · Last updated 5 September 2026

BeeKindBeeYourself Shop Analytics is a private, single-user tool built and operated by the owner of the BeeKindBeeYourself shop. It exists to read analytics for that owner's own Pinterest business account and to publish Pins for that shop's own products. It has no other users, no sign-up, no public interface, and it is not offered to anyone else.

Who this policy covers

One person: the owner of the BeeKindBeeYourself Pinterest business account, who is also the sole operator of this tool. There are no third-party users, customers or visitors whose data this tool could collect.

What data the tool accesses

  • Aggregate performance metrics for the owner's own Pins and boards — impressions, saves, outbound clicks, Pin clicks and engagement.
  • The owner's own Pin and board content — titles, descriptions, alt text, destination links, images and board names.
  • Basic account information for the owner's own Pinterest business account.

The tool does not access other people's Pinterest accounts, Pins, boards, followers or profile information. It does not collect data about anyone who views or interacts with the Pins.

How the data is used

Solely to decide which product designs and Pin layouts to make more of, and to publish Pins for the shop's own products. The data is used for no other purpose.

How the data is stored

Analytics results are written to files on the owner's own computer and to a private repository that only the owner can access. Nothing is stored on a public server. Access credentials are kept in local files that are excluded from version control and are never shared.

What is never done with the data

  • It is never sold, rented or licensed.
  • It is never shared with advertisers, data brokers or any third party.
  • It is never combined with data from other people's Pinterest accounts.
  • It is never used to profile, track or target individual Pinterest users.

Retention and deletion

Analytics files are kept only as long as they are useful for shop decisions and are deleted at the owner's discretion. Pinterest access tokens are stored locally, refreshed as required, and can be revoked at any time from the owner's Pinterest account settings, which immediately ends the tool's access.

Third parties

The tool sends requests only to the Pinterest API. It uses no analytics services, no advertising networks and no third-party trackers. This page itself sets no cookies and collects nothing from visitors.

Changes to this policy

If the tool's scope changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the BeeKindBeeYourself Etsy shop contact form.

BeeKindBeeYourself Shop Analytics is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Pinterest.
